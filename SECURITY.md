# Security

Castpane gives whoever opens Remote control of your Mac, as your macOS user. The full security
model is in [docs/security.md](docs/security.md): who can do what, what Castpane guarantees,
where its secrets are kept, and what it cannot stop. Read it before you open Castpane to the
internet.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem. Email [support@v-ax.com](mailto:support@v-ax.com) instead. You can also use GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on this repository.

Include Castpane, the version or commit, the relevant platform, minimal steps to reproduce, and the expected and observed results. Use fictional data where possible and redact sensitive information from errors or screenshots.

Do not send passwords, tokens, private keys, account data, or raw private logs, and do not send files from `~/Library/Application Support/Castpane`. Include only the information needed to reproduce the problem.

For ordinary usage questions and non-sensitive bugs, use this repository's issue tracker.
